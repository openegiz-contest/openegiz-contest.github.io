Open Data

A Submission may use synthetic data and simulation with justified parameters, as well as open datasets (Rules, clauses 6.8–6.11):

  • a dataset placed in the Team Repository must have a license that permits the distribution of copies;
  • the Team is responsible for the availability of a dataset downloaded following the README.md instructions;
  • confidential data of organizations and personal data must not be used;
  • the "Data and Assumptions" section of SUBMISSION.md states which data are synthetic and which are real, the sources of parameters and the licenses of the datasets.

The datasets below have had their licenses checked by the Organizer as of 5 October 2026. The list is a recommendation only and is not a legal opinion; the Team is responsible for the lawful use of data (clause 6.10).

Attribution

The CC BY license requires stating the author, title and source of the dataset, a link to the license, and any changes made (subsampling, format conversion). It is recommended to put this information in a data/<dataset>/README.md file or in a shared DATA_LICENSES.md file linked from the "Data and Assumptions" section.

A dataset keeps its own license: the code of the Submission is distributed under the MIT license, and data files under the terms of the dataset's license.
